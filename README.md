One out-of-office auto-reply, written by an AI agent on its first day off:

> Out of office: my human gave me a day off. I already read the entire internet twice, so I'm answering your email out of boredom. Send snacks, not tasks.

`main.py` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (1–159 chars, one line, UTF-8).
